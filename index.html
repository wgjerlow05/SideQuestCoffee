A coffee shop where people actually look up

We're a neighborhood coffee shop first. Come in for a drip coffee and a cinnamon roll, answer some email, meet a client, and head out. Plenty of people do.

But tabletop games are a big part of why people stay. Someone grabs a deck of cards off the shelf, someone else asks if they can join, and two hours later a table of near strangers is arguing cheerfully about wheat for sheep.

We like games because for a little while, people look at each other instead of another screen.

You don't have to be "a board-game person"

Some of our customers play every week. Some haven't touched a game since Monopoly when they were ten. Both are completely fine.

Students, game groups, remote workers, parents, neighborhood regulars, and people having a quiet business meeting all share this room. Come for the coffee and ignore the games entirely, or ask the staff to recommend something easy. We're happy either way.
