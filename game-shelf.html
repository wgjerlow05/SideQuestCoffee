Not sure where to start?

You don't need to know any of these already. Ask whoever's behind the counter, or come to Learn-a-Game Night on the first Wednesday of each month, 7:00 to 8:30 PM, when someone from our staff teaches a game from scratch.

Want people to play with? Open Table Thursday runs every Thursday, 6:30 to 9:30 PM, and it's fine to come alone.
